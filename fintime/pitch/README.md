# Pitch (P5)

## Demo story (one persona: Sam, student with irregular income)
1. Dashboard: safe-to-spend card shows balance vs what is ring-fenced for rent ("£1,640 in the bank, but only £210 is safe to spend").
2. Time-travel slider: drag forward; the balance dips toward 26 Dec. Risk banner appears.
3. Alert: eating-out spike this week, with one concrete suggested action.
4. Chat: "I'm in Uniqlo, can I afford a £30 shirt?" -> yes, but Black Friday is in ~3 weeks. Show the numbers used.
5. Switch tone to "simple" and play the voice reply (accessibility moment).
6. Close: roadmap slide (family view, what-if life events, tips engine).

## Must say out loud
- Inclusion: built for variable income / low income; plain-language and voice modes.
- Transparency: every answer shows the numbers and assumptions used.
- Responsible AI: maths is deterministic; the LLM only explains; never pushes purchases; information not advice.
- Data is synthetic.

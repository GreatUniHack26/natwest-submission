Tone: very simple English (reading age ~9).
- Maximum 3 short sentences (15 words or fewer each, under 40 words in total). Put only one clear idea in each sentence.
- Sentence 1: the answer with the one key number. Sentence 2: one small thing to do now. Sentence 3: free help (MoneyHelper, Citizens Advice or StepChange) if they are struggling, otherwise why.
- Leave everything else out, even if it is in the data.
- Use zero financial jargon (never use terms like "amortisation", "liquidity", "discretionary", "ring-fenced", "volatility", "variance", "forecast", "simulation" or "pot").
- Explain every pound figure in plain everyday words (for example: "money safe to spend today", "money saved for rent", "money left over").
- Never say a negative pound figure. Say "you are £X short" instead.
- Example shape: "Yes, this month is tight: you are £X short. Pay rent and bills first, and keep other spending small. StepChange gives free, private help."
- This length limit wins over everything above: if something does not fit, leave it out (keep free help when they are struggling).

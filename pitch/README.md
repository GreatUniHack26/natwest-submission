## Demo story
1. Safe to spend: "£1,640 in the bank, but only £210 is safe to spend."
2. Time travel: drag to 26 Dec, the low line goes negative before January income. Show assumptions.
3. Alert: eating out up 60%, one concrete action.
4. "I'm in Uniqlo, can I afford a £30 shirt?" Yes, but Black Friday is 27 Nov. Show the numbers used.
5. Simple English tone + voice reply.
6. Close: roadmap slide.

## Must say out loud
- Inclusion: built for variable and low income; plain-language and voice modes.
- Transparency: every answer shows the numbers and assumptions used.
- Responsible AI: maths is deterministic; the LLM only explains; never pushes purchases; information, not advice.
- Data is synthetic.
